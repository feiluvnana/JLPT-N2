## 1. Pool growth can retro-FAIL a shipped paper — likely a real defect

`cooldown_for()` re-derives a category's cooldown window from the **current**
pool depth (`len // 5 − 2`), so adding entries retroactively widens the window
every already-shipped paper is judged against.

Measured: adding 4 `paraphrase` entries moved that category 142 → 146 and made
`make check` FAIL with
`20260914_1: rotation claim holds — paraphrase:「案外」 (test 20260807_1, needs its own 27-draw cooldown)`.
Boundary: **≤144 entries keeps the cooldown at 26; 145 breaks it.**

This is why only 2 of 4 verified katakana entries were added. Two more are
measured, justified and **blocked on this**: `テンポ(速さ)` (official 7/2023
問題5-22 key 4 速さ; Hajimete headword 852; Soumatome L2024/2040) and
`プラン(計画)` (Hajimete headword 1021; Shin Kanzen goi L1696).

The spec stamps `pools_sha` precisely so a paper records which pool revision it
was drawn under — and then the rotation check ignores it when re-deriving the
window. Candidate fix: judge a paper's rotation claim against the pool depth its
`pools_sha` names, not today's.

## 2. `ショップ` cannot be removed, and has no N2-band correction

It was **drawn and shipped**: `logs/ledger.json history[0]`,
`tests/20260807_1/test_spec.json items.paraphrase[4]`, printed as that paper's
問題5-25 「駅前に新しいコーヒーの**ショップ**ができた。」 keyed 「お店」 — the
N5-core key the landmine predicts. `exam-blueprint`'s rule (an entry a shipped
paper drew is corrected or kept, never removed) therefore binds.

Correcting it in place IS mechanically possible — `_pool_forms()` strips the
parenthetical, so `ショップ(X)` still resolves the ledger's bare `ショップ` with
no ledger or spec edit — but there is no N2-band synonym to put there: 店 is the
banned N5 core, and 店舗 has **zero hits** in all 31 booklets and all three
vocabulary authorities. Needs a band ruling, not an invented gloss.

## 6. `KUN_TARGET_RATE` encodes a refuted measurement — pipeline decision

`sample_items.py:140` carries `KUN_TARGET_RATE = {"kanji_reading": 12 / 35}` as
a **live sampling constant**, and lines 131–144 restate a per-sitting 「2/2/1/2/2」.
Both are refuted by the repo's own classifier: `is_kun_target()` scores **14 of
35** current-era targets as 訓 — **exactly 2 in every one of the seven
sittings**, never 1. Re-derivable with `is_kun_target()` over the 35
`語(よみ)` pairs in the seven `booklet.md` 問題1 blocks.

Correcting 12/35 → 14/35 **changes every future draw**, and the same recount
argues `KUN_FLOOR` could be 2 rather than 1. That is a pipeline decision, not a
docstring fix, which is why `check_consistency.py:7772`'s docstring was left
alone rather than quietly edited to a third value.

`moji-goi.md` has been made internally consistent and now states the thing that
caused the confusion: **reading type (14/21) and authoring shape (13/22) are two
different measurements**, differing on 背骨 — 訓 by reading, 音読み-shaped by
distractor construction. Do not reconcile them by changing either number.
