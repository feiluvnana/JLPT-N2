Resolution: **reviewer error.** ③「ものでもない」is grammatically restricted to attaching after a NEGATIVE verb form (「〜ないものでもない」, a double-negative retaining possibility, e.g. 行けないものでもない = "not impossible to go") or after という/a potential form; attaching it directly to the bare affirmative 「根づく」, with no という and no degree adverb, is non-standard. ②「はずがない」attaches to a plain/dictionary form directly (「そんなことがあるはずがない」pattern) and correctly expresses the strong negative supposition the paragraph's argument needs (single-visit events → attachment definitely will not take root; 実際, repeated visits → it does). Key stands, but see F7: the paper's own 解説 (`言語知識・読解.md:536`) states only the negative-pairing half of the ものでもない rule and omits the という/potential-form half, so the reasoning as printed is incomplete even though the conclusion is right.
