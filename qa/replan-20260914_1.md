## Why this is allowed at the tail of a run, and what makes it safe

`jlpt-test-generation` §"The fix loop" and `exam-qa-review` §6.5 warn against
bolting a pipeline change onto the end of a generation run — "a diff nobody
reads". The mitigations that make this case different, and they are the same
ones `qa/root-cause-dispositions-20260911-round2.md` used when it re-rendered the
paper under production rather than shipping it with a known defect:

1. **QA has not run yet.** Stage 4 will audit the FINAL bytes, including the
   re-drawn 聴解. Nothing ships unreviewed.
2. **The bank expansion carries its own acceptance checks** (modelled on
   `build_textbook_bank.py`'s guards for non-official sources) and its own
   `make choukai-wear` + `make check` verification, separate from this paper.
3. **The paper is the founding case**, which is the repo's own standard for a
   change of this kind: the rule and its first real exercise ship together.

## AMENDMENT (2026-09-14, after the archive-expansion design landed)

**The re-composition draws from the EXISTING 10-sitting bank, not an enlarged
one.** Everything above about re-drawing the 聴解 half stands; only the pool
does not change. The user was given the measured choice and chose "resample
now, expand after".

Why the pool cannot grow today, measured in
`.agents/choukai-audio/references/archive_bank_expansion.md`:

1. **The archive OCR cannot be banked as-is.** Against hand-verified ground
   truth, archive `script.md` loses ~7 characters per item on content words
   (掲示板→指宗, 診断書→診前善), and **14 % of spoken-option blocks are in the
   wrong ORDER** — the script PDFs lay 問題3/4 options in two columns and the
   OCR reads them visually. Banking that re-keys items, it does not merely
   introduce typos. Every bank text field is learner-visible (the `script` is
   embedded verbatim in `解答.html`'s result screen, `練習.html` and
   `模範解答.html`; `explanation["options"]` IS the printed option list).
2. **The builder's docstring is false.** It claims all 31 sittings run
   5/6/5/11/2. Verified against `refs/JLPT_N2_NEW/answer_keys.json`: 2010-07 …
   2019-12 run 問題4 = 12 and 問題5 = 3 items / 4 answers, some have 問題2 = 5,
   and 2012-12 has 問題3 = 4. **2020-12 is the only un-imported sitting already
   on the modern shape.** The slot-preserving draw assumes that shape, so the
   other 20 need slot mapping whichever route is taken — which also refutes the
   "just import them, no code change" premise.
3. The recommended route (a hand-declared `archive_items.json` third source,
   keys from `key.md` and printed options from `booklet.md`, both exact, every
   dialogue line image-verified) is **~15–25 sessions**, spendable per ITEM so
   it can be aimed at the worst-worn 大問 first. Six of the 21 older sittings are
   on hold on measured grounds: 2019-12 / 2019-07 / 2015-07 (loudness spread),
   2010-12 / 2016-07 (32 kHz).
